[h1]Yayo Animation 1.7 + Tick Fix[/h1]
Original animations by YAYO. Continued version by Soky. Tick Overflow Fix by ifchen0.

[h1]20 October 2026 — Custom Job Facing Fix[/h1]
[list]
[*]Updated the bundled Tick Fix with ifchen0's custom-job facing repair. Stationary pawns now visibly turn when a custom JobDriver changes direction through Pawn.Rotation or FaceTarget().
[*]The repair refreshes only indefinitely cached standing-job results when their facing changes. Scheduled animations and lying poses retain their chosen direction.
[*]Retained the tick-overflow arithmetic correction, explicit-assembly Harmony startup fix, and new-game/load animation-cache resets.
[*]Verified on native RimWorld 1.7.4982 rev1280 / Harmony 3: all 37 native checks passed, including both turning APIs in all four directions before and after private save/reload, preservation of scheduled-animation facing, and 1,080 tick-arithmetic cases.
[/list]
[b]Scope:[/b] The updated DLL targets native RimWorld 1.7. Older version folders are unchanged. Prepatcher-enabled operation and the full player mod stack have not been validated.

Thanks to seeki for the detailed report and to ifchen0 for the upstream repair. Original animation credits remain YAYO and Soky; copyright, source and license notices are retained. Enable this bundled package without also enabling the separate Yayo or Tick Fix copies.

[h1]Changes in this package[/h1]
[list]
[*]RimWorld 1.7 compatibility, including a new-game animation-cache reset correction.
[*]Includes ifchen0's Tick Overflow Fix. Negative animation frames caused by large tick counts or pawn IDs are converted to valid non-negative frames in the standing and laying animation code.
[*]Includes ifchen0's custom-job facing repair for indefinitely cached standing jobs, preserving scheduled-animation and lying-pose facing.
[*]Already-valid remainder results are unchanged. Existing explicit-assembly startup and lifecycle fixes are retained.
[*]Requires Harmony. Enable this package only: do not also enable Yayo's Animation (Continued) or the separate Tick Overflow Fix.
[/list]
The tick correction addresses broken work, social, idle and other job animations, including distorted burning-and-rolling motion. High pawn IDs can trigger the issue even before a colony becomes old.

Tested locally on native RimWorld 1.7.4982 rev1280 / Harmony 3: 37 native checks passed, including both custom-job turning APIs before and after private save/reload, preservation of scheduled-animation facing, and 1,080 arithmetic cases. The updated DLL targets native 1.7; older version folders are unchanged. Prepatcher-enabled operation and the full player mod stack remain untested.

[h1]17 October 2026[/h1]

[b]Update: RimWorld 1.7 Animation Startup Fix[/b]

[list]

[*] Updated Yayo Animation and the bundled Tick Fix to explicitly identify their own assemblies when applying Harmony patches. This addresses a reported issue where automatic assembly detection could silently leave animations unpatched.

[*] Improved the 1.7 compatibility helper to ensure animation-cache resets are applied for both starting a new game and loading an existing save, without duplicating existing hooks.

[*] Preserved the long-running-save tick overflow fix. No animation timing or arithmetic changes in this update.
[/list]
